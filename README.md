# Awesome-Human-Capital-Management-HCM

## Top Human Capital Management (HCM) Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Core HR, Talent Management, Payroll Integration, Workforce Administration, Employee Experience & Enterprise HCM Suites*

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Human Capital Management (HCM)**. These systems manage the full employee lifecycle—from recruiting and onboarding through core HR, performance, learning, compensation, time tracking, and often payroll—serving as the system of record for people data.



**Examples** include Microsoft Dynamics 365 Human Resources, Workday HCM, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle Cloud HCM, ADP Workforce Now, UKG Pro, BambooHR, Paylocity, Rippling, and Ceridian Dayforce (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Enterprise HCM is dominated by commercial suites. Viable open-source options exist for mid-market and self-hosted needs—**Frappe HR**, **OrangeHRM**, and **Odoo HR**—covering core HR, leave, attendance, and limited payroll. This section expands those while remaining realistic about gaps in global payroll, compliance, and scale.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Microsoft Dynamics 365 Human Resources](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics-365/products/human-resources)**  

  Microsoft’s HCM offering integrated with Dynamics 365 and Microsoft 365 for core HR, leave, benefits, and workforce management.



- **[Workday HCM](https://www.workday.com/en-us/products/human-capital-management.html)**  

  Leading cloud HCM suite covering core HR, talent, payroll, workforce planning, and analytics with a strong user experience.



- **[SAP SuccessFactors](https://www.sap.com/products/hcm.html)**  

  Enterprise HCM and talent management suite tightly integrated with the broader SAP ecosystem, strong in global payroll and learning.



- **[Oracle Cloud HCM](https://www.oracle.com/human-capital-management/)**  

  Comprehensive Oracle Fusion HCM covering HR, talent, payroll, and workforce management with deep ERP integration.



- **[ADP Workforce Now](https://www.adp.com/what-we-offer/products/adp-workforce-now.aspx)**  

  Mid-market to enterprise HCM and payroll platform from ADP with broad compliance and benefits capabilities.



- **[UKG Pro](https://www.ukg.com/products/ukg-pro)**  

  Enterprise HCM, workforce management, and payroll suite (formerly Ultimate Software) with strong time and attendance features.



- **[BambooHR](https://www.bamboohr.com/)**  

  Popular mid-market HRIS focused on core HR, onboarding, performance, and employee experience with an intuitive interface.



- **[Paylocity](https://www.paylocity.com/)**  

  Cloud HCM and payroll platform emphasizing modern employee experience, time tracking, and analytics.



- **[Rippling](https://www.rippling.com/)**  

  Unified platform combining HCM, payroll, IT, and spend management for rapidly growing companies.



- **[Ceridian Dayforce](https://www.ceridian.com/products/dayforce)**  

  Continuous-calculation HCM and payroll platform covering workforce management, HR, and talent in a single system.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Frappe HR](https://github.com/frappe/hrms)**  

  Modern open-source HR and payroll software (from the ERPNext/Frappe ecosystem) covering employee lifecycle, leave, attendance, recruitment, performance, and multi-country payroll support.



- **[OrangeHRM](https://github.com/orangehrm/orangehrm)**  

  Long-standing open-source HR management system with modules for employee information, leave, time, recruitment, performance, and more (Community / Starter edition).



- **[Odoo HR](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open-source HR applications within the Odoo suite—employees, recruitment, time off, appraisals, expenses, and integration with broader ERP modules.



- **[ERPNext HR modules](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  HR and payroll capabilities inside ERPNext that complement or overlap with the dedicated Frappe HR product.



- **[Open-source leave and attendance tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Community projects focused on time-off management, shift scheduling, and attendance tracking that can extend core HR systems.



- **[Self-hosted HR portal templates](https://github.com/)**  

  Open frameworks and starter kits for employee self-service portals and basic HRIS functionality.



- **[Documentation and Frappe HR / OrangeHRM playbooks](https://frappehr.com/)**  

  Guides for installing, configuring, and operating open-source HCM stacks in production.



- **[Integration connectors and APIs](https://github.com/)**  

  Community connectors linking open HR systems to payroll providers, SSO, and collaboration tools.



- **[Privacy-preserving people-data patterns](https://github.com/)**  

  Open approaches to handling sensitive employee data with self-hosted systems and strong access controls.



- **[Custom HCM extension frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools and plugins that extend OrangeHRM, Odoo, or Frappe HR for industry-specific or regional needs.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Deploying **Frappe HR** or **OrangeHRM** for core HR, leave, and attendance in mid-sized organizations.

- Using **Odoo HR** when broader ERP capabilities are also required.

- Accepting that global multi-country payroll, complex benefits administration, deep talent suites, and enterprise-scale compliance still favor commercial platforms (Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle Cloud HCM, UKG Pro, ADP, Dayforce, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, customization, and avoiding vendor lock-in for core people records.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Run Frappe HR or OrangeHRM for employee master data and lifecycle → integrate external payroll via APIs → add self-service portals → enforce strong access and audit controls. Suitable for organizations with internal IT capacity. Most large enterprises choose commercial HCM suites for global scale and regulatory coverage.
